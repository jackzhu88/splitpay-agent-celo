SplitPay Agent on Celo

A pay-per-call agent: send it a bill and the people sharing it, it returns exact per-person amounts (no lost cents). Each call costs 0.01 USD₮ via x402. The payment goes to an on-chain RevenueSplitter that forwards revenue to its payees by fixed shares.
